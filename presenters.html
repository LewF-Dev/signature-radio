Presenter

The K-Suite

DJ Kaie

I don’t claim to be the best DJ ever or the King of any Genre. What I do claim is to be a true lover of music & its power, especially through good selection & that’s where I try to pride myself.

So whether it’s an Ol’ Skool RnB trip down memory lane or a Honey filled 9mth Duvet Disco compilation, it will have you feeling exactly how you’re meant to.

Bristol born and bred, Unapologetically Black, I evolved from the days of real house parties, pirate radio stations, blues & late night carnivals & the introduction of the BOX ‘Music You Control’ & MTV.

The feeling that comes with good music is all the drug I need. That extra buzz of seeing the reaction from other people listening to your music selections deep bass and harmonized vocals. Jeeez!!!

Hope I can provide you with a good vibe each time you listen to my selection. Thank you in advance. Enjoy.

Presenter

Urban Dior

Kordova

Kordova is an open-format DJ who refuses to be boxed in.

With a deep-rooted love for all things music, Kordova moves effortlessly across genres, blending sounds in ways that feel both unexpected and completely natural.

Known for an eclectic mix of urban rhythms and global influences, every set is a journey—unpredictable, immersive, and designed to keep the crowd guessing. From soulful grooves to high-energy anthems, Kordova creates moments that invite people to discover something new while staying locked into the vibe.

More than just a DJ, Kordova is a curator of experiences—pushing boundaries, crossing cultures, and proving that great music doesn’t need labels, just an open mind and a willingness to feel it.

Presenter

The BARE Sound Show

Bennjamin Sky

With over 20 years’ experience playing, curating and producing music, Bennjamin Sky operates in spaces where feeling, texture and selection matter.

Shaped by early pirate radio days on Bristol’s Passion FM, his sound is grounded in underground principles and musical integrity.

The BARE Sound Show reflects that ethos, moving through RnB, reggae, soul, funk and world music, with a focus on raw, unfiltered records that sit well outside the algorithm. Expect deep cuts, forgotten gems and music you won’t hear anywhere else.
