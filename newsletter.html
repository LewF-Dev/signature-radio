01 / Station news

The next step in our journey

After three amazing years of broadcasting online, we’re proud to have taken the next step. In June 2026, following a detailed process and meeting all the necessary broadcasting requirements, Signature Radio officially launched on DAB across Bristol and the South West.

To everyone who has listened, supported, shared and followed us over the past three years — thank you. Your support has helped shape Signature Radio into what it is today. Many of you have now joined us on DAB, while our online and smart-device streams continue as before.
